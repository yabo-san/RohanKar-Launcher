# The user loop

The definition lives in [AGENT-BRIEF.md](AGENT-BRIEF.md#the-user-loop). In short:

1. **Add.** Paste a link into Add. An archive.org link adds one upload or a whole account; a
   GitHub or GitLab link adds a repo.
2. **Browse.** The game wall shows your archive.org sources, Ports shows your repos and the
   built-in picks. Right-click, Hide from Wall, leaves an upload out.
3. **Install.** One click. A repo installs its latest release's Windows build. An archive.org
   zip, 7z or rar is extracted and playable when the upload is shaped like Rohan's (one game,
   ready to run); anything else just downloads, and the app is an archive.org download manager.
4. **Play.** Play, Add to Steam, Open folder, uninstall to the Recycle Bin, Playnite export.
5. **Update.** A badge shows when there's something newer; one click updates. Nothing updates on
   its own.

Curation is not the point: the app ships with a few starting sources and vouches for nothing.
